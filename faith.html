Christian Faith

A lifelong foundation.

Raised in the Christian faith, Kurtis describes his relationship with Jesus Christ and trust in God as foundational to his life. His faith informs his approach to family, leadership, charity and service.

Christian faith is not presented here simply as a title or affiliation, but as a continuing commitment expressed through worship, ministry, charitable service and responsibility to others.
Ministry

Anglican ministry & Christian service.

The Rev. Kurtis P. Orcutt serves in Anglican ministry through the United Anglican Church. His ministry work complements his wider involvement in Christian charitable and chivalric organizations.
