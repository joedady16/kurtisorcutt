Personal Biography

A career and vocation spanning more than four decades.

Kurtis P. Orcutt began his automotive career in Phoenix, Arizona, in 1983. He progressed through sales, finance, dealership management, corporate training and national account leadership before moving into senior executive roles in vehicle protection products and related transportation industries.

Alongside his business career, Kurtis has pursued Christian ministry, charitable and chivalric service, historical study and international fellowship. These interests have taken him into leadership roles with organizations in the United States and abroad and have included repeated travel to the Holy Land.

Education

Academic Record

Kurtis holds a Bachelor’s degree and a Master of Business Administration. In May 2010, he received an honorary Doctorate in Business Administration, reflected in his ceremonial styling as DBA.

Family

Faith, family & legacy.

Kurtis and Margarita Orcutt were married on August 15, 2024. Family is presented as an integral part of the site's story rather than as a separate résumé item—reflecting the importance of faith, commitment and legacy in Kurtis's life.

Historical Interests

History & the Holy Land.

Kurtis's interests include Christian history, chivalric traditions, archaeology and historical travel. The photographic archive includes Jerusalem, the Holy Sepulchre, the Garden Tomb, Emmaus, Castillum Arnaldi, Byzantine sites and Hellenistic-period caves.
